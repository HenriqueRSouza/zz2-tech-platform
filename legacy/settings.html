Sync & store

Ticket store
Failed writes
Ticket saves that exhausted their retries to the Google Sheet. The local store already reflects these, so they're the rows where the store and the sheet can diverge. Replay re-queues them for the write-behind worker; Clear discards them (a rebuild will still resync from the sheet).
Maintenance
Auto-resolve stale tickets
Tickets with a blank status or a “Waiting for customer feedback” status that are older than 7 days get marked Resolved in the Google Sheet. Anything newer is left untouched, so active work is never closed. This runs automatically each night; change the threshold under Settings.
